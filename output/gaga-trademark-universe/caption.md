# Caption — Lady Gaga's Trademark Universe (Instagram / LinkedIn)

Lady Gaga's stage name is only the center of the map. U.S. trademark records filed by Ate My Heart Inc. — the company behind her brands — span 65 filings and registrations across 18 distinct marks: HAUS LABS cosmetics, the LITTLE MONSTERS community, personas like JO CALDERONE, era brands like ARTPOP, even wine.

The lesson for founders: a valuable brand is a portfolio, not a single name. Map the core mark, the products, the community, and the campaigns — then protect each on its own timeline.

The facts above reflect the supplied USPTO records; statuses vary by record, and several filings shown are no longer active. The export may not represent the complete worldwide portfolio.
