# Caption — Nicki Minaj's Trademark Strategy

Nicki Minaj's records hold a clean strategy lesson: 3 live registrations, 3 pending applications, 29 records no longer active. What survived is exactly where the products shipped.

The 2012 wave reserved NICKI MINAJ across 20 categories and none completed. The beauty line registered and stayed. And in March 2024, three PINK FRIDAY intent-to-use applications reserved nail products ahead of launch, with everything held by Little Miss Mogul, LLC.

Takeaway: register where revenue is real, reserve names just before launches, and let speculative coverage go.
