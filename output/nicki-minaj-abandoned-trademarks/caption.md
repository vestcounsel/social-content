# Caption — Nicki Minaj's Abandoned Trademarks

Nicki Minaj's records include 27 abandoned applications, and every one was an intent-to-use reservation. The famous example: a November 2012 spree that filed NICKI MINAJ across 20 categories, from flatware to restaurants. All 20 lapsed.

The lesson founders miss: abandoned describes the application, not the brand. Five fragrance names were explored through filings that never finished. None of that tells you why, and none of it makes a name free. Live registrations, common-law use, and refilings can still block adoption.

Takeaway: calendar every deadline, and treat any abandoned record as a question to investigate.
