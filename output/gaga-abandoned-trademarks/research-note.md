# Internal research note — Lady Gaga's Abandoned Trademarks

**Strategic angle:** misconception correction — what "abandoned" does and does not mean.
**Audience:** founders and creators tempted to adopt "abandoned" names; educational.

## Scope discipline (Series 3 vs Series 4)
This carousel uses ONLY dead records with **no registration number** — i.e., applications that ended before registration (abandoned applications). Registrations that issued and later died are excluded here and covered in Series 4.

## Legal-accuracy guardrails on the slides
- No reason is assigned to any specific abandonment; slide 4 lists general possibilities only ("a statement of use goes unfiled, a deadline passes, plans shift, or a filing is replaced").
- Availability warning is explicit (slide 6): common-law use, related live registrations, other rights.
- "Abandonment is usually procedural" (slide 7) is a general statement about how abandonment occurs mechanically, not a claim about these records.

## Why it may perform
Corrects a genuinely common founder mistake with a famous, searchable example set.
