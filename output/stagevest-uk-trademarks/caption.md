# Caption — Why Stagevest Filed Two UK Trademarks

Stagevest, a professional networking and collaboration platform for the music industry, now holds two UK trademark registrations. One protects the name, the word mark STAGEVEST in Class 42. The other protects the sound-wave logo, covering the same core software, networking, and music-industry platform services.

Why both? A word mark protects the brand name itself, and a figurative mark protects the visual design used to represent it. Together they can create broader and more flexible protection as a company grows.

Takeaway: a trademark portfolio should reflect how customers actually encounter the brand. That may include the company name, the product name, the logo, a distinctive icon, a tagline, and the names of major features.
