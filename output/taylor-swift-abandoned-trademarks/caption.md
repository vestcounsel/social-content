# Caption — Taylor Swift's Abandoned Trademarks

Even Taylor Swift's portfolio includes 36 abandoned applications, most from the famous October 2014 lyric filings. THIS SICK BEAT alone was filed in 16 categories; 13 of those applications went abandoned.

Here is what founders get wrong: abandoned describes the application, not the brand. Nearly all of these were intent-to-use reservations, placeholders that only mature into registrations once real use is proven. And an abandoned filing does not make a name available. Related registrations, common-law rights, and new filings can still block adoption.

Takeaway: calendar your deadlines, and treat any abandoned record as the start of a question, not clearance.
