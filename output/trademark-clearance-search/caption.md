# Caption — How Trademark Clearance Works

A trademark clearance search answers a bigger question than "is this name taken?" Conflicts come from similarity in sound, appearance, meaning, or overall impression, paired with related goods or services, and class numbers do not draw those lines.

The searches that burn founders are the shortcuts: a registered domain is not clearance, an approved LLC name is not clearance, and a search that stops at the federal database misses state records and common-law rights that arise from marketplace use alone.

Takeaway: run the search before the domains, packaging, inventory, and ad spend, while changing course is still cheap. A search assesses risk; it cannot guarantee registration.

Trademark status and availability depend on the complete record and current marketplace. This content is for general educational purposes and is not legal advice.
