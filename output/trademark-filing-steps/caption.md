# Caption — 5 Steps Before You File

A trademark application is not a reservation form. It is a legal submission with factual representations about ownership, use, and the goods behind the mark, and some mistakes, like naming the wrong owner, cannot be fixed after filing.

Five steps before you submit: confirm who actually owns the mark, run a real clearance search, describe what customers buy (a winery sells wine, not labels), pick the right filing basis, and gather genuine specimens and per-class fees. You can narrow an application later; you cannot broaden it.

Takeaway: the filing succeeds or fails before you ever open Trademark Center.

Trademark rights, filing requirements, and registration prospects depend on the specific facts and current legal record. This content is for general educational purposes and is not legal advice.
