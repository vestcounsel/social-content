# Caption — Lady Gaga's Dead Trademarks (LinkedIn / Instagram)

Lady Gaga's records include 52 "dead" trademarks — and that word means less than most people think. Dead is a database label that covers very different endings: in the supplied files, 42 applications ended before registering, while 10 registrations issued and only later left the register — including the LITTLE MONSTERS community marks and ARTPOP merchandise.

Meanwhile, a HAUS LABORATORIES registration that shows dead in one record is alive in a newer one. A single dead record is not proof a brand failed — and it is never proof a name is available. Use can continue, common-law rights can persist, and related live filings can exist.

Takeaway: portfolios are living systems. Read the whole record, not one status line, before you rely on it.
