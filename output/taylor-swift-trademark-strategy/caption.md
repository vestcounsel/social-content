# Caption — Taylor Swift's Trademark Strategy

Taylor Swift's trademark records show a discipline worth copying: all 66 live registrations were use-based. The register mirrors what actually ships.

The pattern in the filings: the TAYLOR SWIFT name registered first (2008), coverage arrived in coordinated waves timed to album cycles, and layers of marks (name, initials, era titles) closed the gaps. Coverage that stopped earning its place, like one SPEAK NOW website registration, was allowed to go while seven others stayed live.

Takeaway: register the core early, layer around it, and prune on a schedule.
