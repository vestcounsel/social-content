**Structure:** cover + setup + one slide per mistake + closing. Mistake numbers not displayed (standing no-numbering rule); subheadings carry each principle ("Fanciful beats descriptive", "AURORA software, not an Aurora").

**Accuracy checkpoints:**
- "Creamy for yogurt" is the article's own descriptiveness example.
- Registration-symbol point phrased as "before registration erodes a brand"; the article's fuller nuance (®  only for covered goods/services) lives here and in the article.
- Maintenance windows stated as the article gives them (Section 8 between years 5-6; combined 8 and 9 each decade); Madrid-based registrations differ, noted in the article only.
