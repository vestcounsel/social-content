# Caption — 5 Trademark Mistakes To Avoid

Most trademark problems trace back to five decisions. Choosing a name that describes instead of distinguishes. Falling in love with it before searching. Filing with the wrong owner, wrong goods, or a mockup specimen. Using the mark inconsistently, or as a generic noun. And forgetting that registrations need maintenance filings between years five and six, then every decade.

Registration is one part of protection, not the whole strategy. The brand becomes an asset when selection, clearance, filing, use, and upkeep all pull in the same direction.

Takeaway: fixing these early is cheap. Fixing them after a launch, a funding round, or a missed federal deadline is not.

Trademark rights and registration requirements depend on the complete facts, current marketplace, and applicable legal record. This content is for general educational purposes and is not legal advice.
